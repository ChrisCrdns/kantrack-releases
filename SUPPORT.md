# KanTrack Support

For help with KanTrack, please open a GitHub issue:

https://github.com/ChrisCrdns/kantrack-releases/issues

When asking for help, include:

- Your macOS version.
- Your KanTrack version.
- What you expected to happen.
- What happened instead.
- Any steps that reliably reproduce the issue.

Do not include private task contents, exported board files, or other sensitive information in public issues.
